Features & Use Cases · 02

Five AI components. Seven business outcomes.

A focused stack of agentic AI components powers every downstream use case — from quality audits to cross-sell. Independently observable, jointly composable, built for the ADIB context.

AI Components

Core building blocks
AI·01

Language Identification & Transcripts

Whisper-based transcription tuned for ADIB's call mix: 90% Arabic, 10% English. Transcripts power every downstream module.

AI·02

Speaker Diarization

Distinguishes agent from customer turn-by-turn. Foundation for interaction scoring, sentiment, and hold analytics.

AI·03

Turn-by-turn emotional trajectory for customer and agent. Surfaces escalation risk and satisfaction signals.

AI·04

Concise call narratives ready for case files, supervisor review, and follow-up routing. Searchable across the full corpus.

AI·05

Key Conversation Highlights

Structured metrics extracted from every conversation: priority, escalation flags, intent, eligibility, and quality indicators.

Solution Architecture · 03

From recorded call to manager dashboard.

A Kafka-Spark streaming pipeline with batch backup handles every call. Agentic AI components enrich each conversation with sentiment, escalation signals, and structured summaries — flowing into the data lake and surfacing on dashboards and leaderboards.

Architectural Stack

Real-time + Batch · Agentic AI · DataLake
L·01

Ingestion · Call Centre to Storage

Call centre agent conversations recorded and pulled continuously into the pipeline. Lossless retention with bank-grade encryption at rest and in motion.

L·02

Processing · Kafka + Spark

Kafka and Spark stream calls in real time for time-sensitive analytics. Spark and Python batch pipelines handle the full retrospective corpus.

L·03

Speech-to-Text · Arabic & English

Custom-built for the ADIB context. 90% of calls are Arabic, 10% English. Transcription accuracy reached 80% on Arabic over 4 iterations of manual validation.

L·04

Storage · Data Store + DataLake

Operational data store powers low-latency serving. DataLake retains the full enriched corpus for retrospective analytics, audit, and model training.

L·05

Agentic AI · Four Specialised Components

Sentiment & Priority Scoring · Escalation Detection · Conversation Summary · Data Validation & Crafting. Each agent is independently observable, version-controlled, and feeds the serving layer that drives the manager dashboard and agent leaderboard.
