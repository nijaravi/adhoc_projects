Slide 03 / Agent Performance Analytics

Six signals. One fair score.

Every agent is evaluated across four dimensions of service quality. These resolve into six weighted scores, which combine into one final agent score — comparable across teams, language, and call mix.

01
Interaction Quality

AI analyses the transcript to rate the agent's tone, clarity, helpfulness, and professionalism.

02
Call Handling Efficiency

Measures how effectively the agent manages call time and resolves customer needs.

03
Hold Management

Tracks how often and how long customers are placed on hold, reflecting call control and efficiency.

04
Operational Consistency

Uses aggregated data across all calls to provide a fair, stable view of the agent's performance.

Six Scoring Components

Each scored 0 – 100 · Weighted into one final
0.45
Weight

Interaction Score

Interaction Quality

Composite of tone, clarity, helpfulness, and professionalism — extracted from the transcript by the analytics agent. The heaviest weight in the formula.

Volume of calls handled relative to peers in the same skill group, adjusted for shift length and call mix.

Call Handling Efficiency

Average Handle Time benchmarked against the optimal band for the call category — too fast and too slow both penalised.

0.15
Weight

Hold Ratio Score

Hold Management

Share of calls that required a hold — lower is better, indicating the agent resolves more in-line without parking the customer.

0.10
Weight

Hold Duration Score

Hold Management

Average length of hold across calls — measures how quickly the agent returns to the customer once a hold is placed.

0.05
Weight

Hold Frequency Score

Hold Management

Number of holds within a single call — frequent toggling signals friction and erodes the customer experience.

Final Agent Score · Weighted Average
= 0.45 Interaction + 0.10 Productivity + 0.15 AHT + 0.15 Hold Ratio + 0.10 Hold Duration + 0.05 Hold Frequency
